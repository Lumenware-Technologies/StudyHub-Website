Our Primary School Facilities

Our primary school is equipped with age-appropriate facilities designed to support young learners. From safe transportation to colorful play areas and nutritious dining, we create a welcoming environment where primary school children can learn, play, and grow.

Playground

Spacious playgrounds with age-appropriate equipment where children can run, climb, and play safely during break times and physical education classes.

Healthy Canteen

Nutritious meals and child-friendly snacks are served daily, providing the energy growing children need to learn and play throughout the school day.

Positive Learning

Bright, engaging classrooms with interactive learning resources that make education fun and help young learners develop a lifelong love of learning.

Enriching Primary Education Beyond The Classroom

At Study Hub Montessori, we believe every child deserves a well-rounded education. Beyond core subjects like English, Mathematics, and Science, we offer exciting activities that develop creativity, teamwork, and confidence.

Our primary school children participate in art projects, music lessons, sports days, school plays, and cultural celebrations. These experiences help young learners discover their talents and build friendships in a supportive environment.
