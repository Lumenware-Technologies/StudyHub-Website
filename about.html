Who We Are

StudyHub Montessori School

StudyHub Montessori School was birthed on October 5, 2020. We are a pure Montessori school equipped with relevant materials to facilitate hands-on learning.

Our enriched, prepared and stimulating environment coupled with a comprehensive curriculum and a team of painstaking teachers make learning spontaneous, easy and fun for our children.

Our Vision

Christ-centered education, empowering a generation that is socially responsible and better equipped in making positive difference in their family, community and the world.

Our Mission

  • To operate exemplary schools offering the highest quality and authentic Montessori education.
  • To educate children using best practices, operational excellence and a culture of integrity.
  • To provide international standard teacher training and short courses for parents, professionals, etc.
What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Cheerful

Caring

We cater to, and support our children, parents, one another and families.

Integrity
